Starting a Loudr campaign begins with three things: your TikTok sound link, the number of posts you want and any instructions for your campaign.

## Send your TikTok sound link

Share the link to the specific sound you want used in the campaign. If your music has more than one version, let us know which one you want to promote.

## Choose your campaign size

Tell us how many posts you have in mind. Our team will review your request and confirm the campaign requirements and estimated delivery period before work begins.

## Share your creative direction

You can leave the creative approach open or provide instructions. Let us know if you have a preferred format, text to include or a video you would like us to use. We’ll tell you what’s possible.

## Know what the campaign delivers

Loudr creates and publishes the agreed number of posts using your music across real TikTok accounts. More posts give people more opportunities to discover your sound, but views, engagement and streams are not guaranteed.
